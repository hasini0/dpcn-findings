Big teams

A quarter of all papers have ten or more authors, many of them CERN experiments and other consortia. Those papers are highly cited and appear in top journals, so they can make an institute look stronger than its own labs are. Removing them is the quickest test.

That is why the dashboard has < 10 authors and < 12 authors views, and why people-level results (best papers, research groups) always use small-team papers.
