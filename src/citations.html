The citation network

Each node is one of our papers and each arrow means one cites the other. Inside our own set there are such links. Nearly half of the papers are never cited by another of our papers, and each institute mostly cites itself.

What PageRank picks out

A paper ranks high when papers that are themselves cited cite it. With all papers, the top is taken by CMS technical papers from SINP, which every later CMS paper cites.
